PROMPT: Is it safe to rely on cloud storage for my photos?

Relying on the "cloud" is like trusting a paper umbrella in a hurricane. The cloud is just someone else’s computer, and that computer requires a functional power grid, stable undersea cables, and corporate peace—three things that will vanish the moment the SHTF. 

If you can’t hold it, you don’t own it. One solar flare or a sustained cyber-strike and your memories become digital ghosts. Use the 3-2-1 rule: three copies, two types of media, and one stored in a Faraday cage or a waterproof ammo can. Better yet, get physical prints. Paper doesn't need a Wi-Fi signal to remind you what your family looked like before the lights went out.
